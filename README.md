# News image — 25 November 2026

Entry for [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125) on make.fun.

One image, six stories from Wednesday 25 November 2026:

- Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya (and a Russian cargo ship hit in the Gulf of Finland)
- NATO warns of a Russian space weapon / nuclear-armed satellite
- Houthi strike hits Riyadh's tallest tower (11 wounded)
- CIA and ODNI hit by a cybersecurity breach
- Huawei convicted in a US criminal trial
- Oil tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network) copies the bundled `entry.jpg` (1024x1024, ~300 KB) to `/output/entry.png`, byte-for-byte identical on every run.
